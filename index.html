      How to play

      • Objective. Reach the end of five sectors with hull remaining and at least three Harvesters destroyed. Hull at zero or fewer than three kills at the terminus ends the campaign.
      • Movement. Match the side limits with the shift, then set the node to their common value. Press Enter to weld before the train reaches the boundary.
      • Combat. Use Kinetic Piercer against armor and fire when the rate meets the shield’s threshold. Use Stasis Sniper against phase shields and fire at |θ′| < 0.25 rad/s. The pulse uses the rate at launch and deals damage on impact. Each surviving Harvester costs 24 hull at the exit.
      • Touch. Use the sliders or +/− buttons to adjust the rail, then tap Engage track weld. Switch ammo or fire from the bar at the bottom of the screen.
      • Keys. W/S or ↑/↓ change k. A/D or ←/→ change the node. Enter welds and Space fires. Tab moves through the controls.
